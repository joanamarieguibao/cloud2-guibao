About ECOAST

Learn. Create.
Connect.

ECOAST is centered on learning through curiosity, collaboration, technology, and practical problem-solving at Panpacific University North Philippines.

Urdaneta Campus Panpacific University

ECOAST connects different areas of engineering, computing, science, and technology so students can understand how classroom knowledge can be applied to real situations.

Through projects, activities, collaboration, and hands-on learning, students are encouraged to develop technical skills while exploring their interests.

  • Engineering and problem solving
  • Computing and digital skills
  • Science and innovation
  • Technology and practical solutions

Explore the degree programs under ECOAST at Panpacific University North Philippines – Urdaneta Campus. Each program develops technical knowledge, practical skills, and problem-solving abilities.

01 / INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY </>

BSIT

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Focuses on information technology, software, systems, databases, networks, and digital solutions for different organizational and real-world needs.

02 / CIVIL ENGINEERING ◇

BSCE

Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering

Focuses on the planning, design, construction, and maintenance of structures and infrastructure through engineering principles and problem solving.

03 / COMPUTER SCIENCE { }

BSCS

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Develops skills in computing, programming, algorithms, software development, and computational problem solving.

04 / ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING ⚡

BSEE

Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering

Focuses on electrical systems, circuits, power, electronics, and engineering applications involving electricity and energy.

05 / COMPUTER ENGINEERING ⚙

Combines computing and engineering concepts involving computer hardware, software, embedded systems, and technology-based solutions.
