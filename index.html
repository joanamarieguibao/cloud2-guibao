The ECOAST mindset / 01

At ECOAST, learning is active, collaborative, and deeply connected to real life. Our students are encouraged to explore the why behind every how, then turn insight into something useful.

Whether they are designing a solution, writing their first program, investigating a scientific question, or building with new technology, they learn to think clearly and create responsibly.

Still wondering where to begin? Here are a few helpful starting points.

Who is ECOAST for?

ECOAST is for curious learners who want to explore engineering, computing, science, and technology in a connected, hands-on environment.

What makes the ECOAST approach different?

We connect ideas across disciplines and place making at the center of learning. Students are encouraged to ask questions, work with others, and learn through iteration.

How can I learn more or arrange a visit?

Send a note to our admissions team using the button above. They can help you learn more and guide you through the next steps.
